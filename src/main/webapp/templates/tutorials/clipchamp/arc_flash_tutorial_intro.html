What is an arc flash?

A short circuit can ionize the air into plasma. In milliseconds, thermal energy reaches the worker. Incident energy (cal/cm²) at the working distance decides PPE — not the bolted fault current alone.

1
Ibf
Bolted 3-phase fault current from short-circuit study (kA)
2
Iarc
Arcing current — lower than Ibf due to arc impedance
3
IE
Incident energy at working distance (cal/cm²)
4
AFB
Arc-flash boundary — distance where IE = 1.2 cal/cm²
5
PPE
NFPA 70E category from incident energy

Why clearing time matters

  • IE scales strongly with arc duration
  • Evaluate both Iarc and Iarc-min (IEEE 1584)
  • Longer TCC at reduced current → often worst case

PPE from incident energy

  • < 1.2 cal/cm² → Cat 0
  • 1.2–4 → Cat 1 · 4–8 → Cat 2
  • 8–25 → Cat 3 · 25–40 → Cat 4
  • ≥ 40 → Dangerous / special PPE

IEEE 1584-2018 — what Electrisim needs

Valid for 208 V – 15 kV. Above 15 kV Electrisim uses the Ralph Lee method and flags it on the result box.

Inputs in the dialog

  • Electrode configuration (VCB … HOA)
  • Working distance (mm) — typical LV 455 mm
  • Conductor gap & enclosure H × W × D
  • Clearing time at Iarc and Iarc-min

Results on each bus

  • IE [cal/cm²] — incident energy
  • AFB [mm] — arc-flash boundary
  • PPE category
  • Ia and Ikss [kA]
