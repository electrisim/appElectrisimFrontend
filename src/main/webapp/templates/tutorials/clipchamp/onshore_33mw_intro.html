Network

33 MW farm at the 110 kV POC

Two 30 kV collector strings feed a 40 MVA 110/30 kV substation. A 5 km 110 kV cable reaches the grid-operator bus. A shunt reactor sits on the 110 kV substation bus — farm side of the POC.

Plant (ours to control)

  • 10 × 3.3 MVA WTGs at 0.69 kV
  • 10 × 3.75 MVA 30/0.65 kV padmounts
  • Two 30 kV strings, 5 turbines each
  • 40 MVA 110/30 kV main transformer
  • Shunt reactor at substation 110 kV

Grid interface

  • POC = Point of connection — 110 kV
  • 5 km 110 kV cable to the POC
  • External grid voltage 1.1 pu (this case)
  • Park controller measures Q at the POC
  • Everything grid-side of the POC is TSO

Results at the POC

High voltage, underexcited PF 0.94

This snapshot is the leading / U = 1.1 pu case. The farm generates 25 MW; 24.28 MW reaches the POC. The park controller absorbs 8.80 Mvar — matching the cosφ(P) characteristic.

POC · External Grid

  • P = 24.28 MW export
  • Q = 8.80 Mvar absorbed
  • PF = 0.94 underexcited
  • U = 1.100 pu (121.0 kV)

Plant internals

  • 10 × 2.50 MW / −0.743 Mvar
  • Substation 110 kV: 1.101 pu
  • Collector 30 kV: 1.07–1.08 pu
  • Main transformer loading 59.9%
